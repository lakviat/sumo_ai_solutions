Questions

Good questions, honest answers.

Don't see yours? Just ask. We're happy to explain anything.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs. A simple site costs less than one with booking or online ordering. After a short conversation, you get a clear price and timeline before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. Tell us about your business and your customers in your own words. We'll turn that into a plan and handle the technical parts.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes. We can refresh the design, fix what isn't working, make it faster and phone-friendly, or rebuild it if that's the better option. We'll tell you honestly which makes more sense.

Will my website work on phones?

Always. Most of your customers will see your website on a phone first, so we design for phones from the very beginning.

Do you only work with businesses in Miami?

No. We're based in Miami, Florida, and work with businesses everywhere. Everything can be done online.

What do I need to get started?

Just an idea. Send us your name, email, and a few sentences about your business. If you have a logo, photos, or websites you like, you can attach or link them, but that's optional.
